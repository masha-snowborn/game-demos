PILLS & THRILLS / HOW TO PLAY

Steady on.
Party later.

  1. Let’s go. Choose a whole-credit bet and start. Granny waits with her walker at the first step and carefully descends one step at a time. Each safe step raises your multiplier.
  2. Catch the moment. After the first safe step, cash out at any time. Your bet × the last completed step’s multiplier is your payout. Granny celebrates with a disco dance.
  3. A spirited exit. If she tumbles before you collect, the payout is zero. She rolls down the stairs, lands on her back and her soul flies upward and leaves the screen. Press ONE MORE LIFE to bring her soul back from above the screen: light spreads from her body across the whole screen, then clears to reveal her standing with the walker at the next staircase. Resurrection costs no credits and starts no bet. Press Another run to place the next bet. At 1,000×, collection is automatic.

Same mathematics as Dish Happens

RTP 98% · multiplier growth 10% · maximum 1,000× · 73 steps.

Integer quotes: q₀ = 100; qₙ₊₁ = min(100000, ceil(qₙ × 1.10)). First-step survival is 98 / 110; later it is qₙ / qₙ₊₁. Each permitted completed stopping point has 98% theoretical return.

The falling step is chosen at the start using the browser’s cryptographic random generator. Bets: 1–1,000,000 whole CR. Payouts use 0.01 CR. An active run does not resume after reload.
