DISH HAPPENS / GAME RULES

Catch the moment.
Save the dinner.

  1. Let him roll. Each run deducts your bet. Manny steadies his plates, then skates and steps across the banquet tables. Each safe landing quickens the movement and background music.
  2. Watch your step. Each safe landing raises the multiplier. A table can collapse on the first transfer. A fall pays zero. Your bet × the multiplier printed on the cloth determines the round payout.
  3. Cash out. After the first safe landing, you can cash out even while Manny is stepping. Your bet × the last reached multiplier determines the payout. Cash out closes when the round ends.

The original game's mathematics

RTP: 98% · growth: 10% · maximum: 1,000×

Multipliers use integer hundredths: q₀ = 100,
qₙ₊₁ = min(100000, ⌈qₙ × 1.10⌉).

The first-step survival chance is 98 / 110. For subsequent steps it is qₙ / qₙ₊₁. The theoretical return for every permitted completed stopping strategy is therefore 98% of the bet.

The hidden falling step is chosen when the run starts and revealed at impact. At the maximum multiplier, the payout is automatic. Bets are whole credits from 1 to 1,000,000; payouts use 0.01-credit units.

Source: BottleStairs.dll and .mathproject.json embedded in the supplied application. The formula was ported from the module's IL code. The demo uses the browser's cryptographic random generator; the original RNG sequences are not reproduced.
